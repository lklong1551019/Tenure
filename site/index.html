Tenure Releases Privacy

Tenure

A macOS app for keeping a record of what you did in your career: one board per company and time span, and for each task the problem, why it mattered, the solution, the impact, the difficulties, and the numbers that prove it.

Your data stays in a file on your Mac. Optional Google Drive sync keeps a copy in a file the app creates in your own Drive, so your Macs stay in step.
