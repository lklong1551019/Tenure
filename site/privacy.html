Tenure Web app Releases Privacy

Tenure - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026 (web app added)

Tenure is a macOS app that keeps a record of your work history in a file on your Mac, and a web app that opens the same file from your Google Drive in a browser. Neither has a server or an account system of its own. This page explains what they do with your Google data.

What the app accesses

  • Google Drive, limited to files the app creates (scope drive.file, the only scope the app requests). The app creates one file, tenure.json, in a "Tenure" folder in your Drive, and reads and updates only that file. It cannot see any other file in your Drive, your email address, or your profile.

How your data is handled

  • The file in your Drive is the same plain JSON file the app keeps on your Mac. It is not encrypted by the app; it is protected by your Google account like any other file in your Drive.
  • The Google sign-in token is stored in the macOS Keychain on your Mac only. It is never uploaded and never sent anywhere except to Google to authorize Drive requests.

Tenure on the web

  • The web app at lklong1551019.github.io/Tenure/app/ runs entirely in your browser. It talks directly to Google's sign-in and to Google Drive; there is no Tenure server in between.
  • It asks for the same single permission (drive.file) and reads and updates only the tenure.json file the Mac app created. It never creates other files.
  • The sign-in token lasts about an hour and is kept only in the open page's memory. It is gone when you close the tab or sign out, and is never written to the browser's storage or sent anywhere except to Google.
  • Your data is held in the page's memory while it is open and is not stored in the browser. The page remembers two display settings (grid or list, and sort order) in the browser's local storage; nothing else.
  • No cookies, analytics, or tracking. The page loads Google's sign-in script from accounts.google.com. GitHub Pages serves the page's files and, like any web host, sees an ordinary request from your network address; it never sees your data.

What the app does not do

  • No analytics, tracking, advertising, or crash reporting.
  • No data is sent to the developer or to any third party. The app talks to Google's sign-in and Drive services, and to GitHub to check for a newer version.
  • Your data is never sold, shared, or used for any purpose other than syncing your own file between your own Macs and showing it to you on the web.

Update checks

Every 12 hours, and when you choose Check for Updates, the app downloads the public list of released versions from raw.githubusercontent.com. The request carries nothing about you or your data; GitHub sees an ordinary web request from your network address. Turn it off with "Check for updates every 12 hours" in the Software Update window.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Tenure's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and data

  • Sign out in the app's settings or on the web page, or revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The Mac app and the web app are signed out separately.
  • Delete the "Tenure" folder from your Google Drive to remove the copy. The data on your Mac is unaffected.
