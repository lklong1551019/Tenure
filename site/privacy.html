Tenure Releases Privacy

Tenure - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026 (update checks added)

Tenure is a macOS app that keeps a record of your work history in a file on your Mac. It has no server and no account system of its own. This page explains what the app does with your Google data when you turn on Google Drive sync.

What the app accesses

  • Google Drive, limited to files the app creates (scope drive.file, the only scope the app requests). The app creates one file, tenure.json, in a "Tenure" folder in your Drive, and reads and updates only that file. It cannot see any other file in your Drive, your email address, or your profile.

How your data is handled

  • The file in your Drive is the same plain JSON file the app keeps on your Mac. It is not encrypted by the app; it is protected by your Google account like any other file in your Drive.
  • The Google sign-in token is stored in the macOS Keychain on your Mac only. It is never uploaded and never sent anywhere except to Google to authorize Drive requests.

What the app does not do

  • No analytics, tracking, advertising, or crash reporting.
  • No data is sent to the developer or to any third party. The app talks to Google's sign-in and Drive services, and to GitHub to check for a newer version.
  • Your data is never sold, shared, or used for any purpose other than syncing your own file between your own Macs.

Update checks

Every 12 hours, and when you choose Check for Updates, the app downloads the public list of released versions from raw.githubusercontent.com. The request carries nothing about you or your data; GitHub sees an ordinary web request from your network address. Turn it off with "Check every 12 hours" in the Software Update window.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Tenure's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and data

  • Sign out in the app's settings, or revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Delete the "Tenure" folder from your Google Drive to remove the copy. The data on your Mac is unaffected.
